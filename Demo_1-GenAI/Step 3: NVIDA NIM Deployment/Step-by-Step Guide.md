# Step 3: NVIDIA NIM Deployment (Llama Model)
This step will demonstrate how to deploy an NVIDIA NIM on our GPU nodes within our OpenShift Cluster. This NVIDIA NIM will be running Llama3 to generate summarizes of our report narratives.
